2. Refining a Research Topic
•	Eligibility screening: Reviewing search records against strict eligibility criteria to eliminate imprecise material.
•	Scoping and boundary definition: Managing project scope by defining explicit boundaries of time, tasks, and people.
•	Maintaining research boundaries: Establishing and maintaining the research boundary to align variables within a research framework.
•	Defining domains of interest: Formulating and explicitly defining the domain of interest of the researchers to avoid irrelevant information retrieval.
