7. Research Ethics
•	Psychosocial risk mitigation: Examining stress, discrimination, heightened precariousness, and work intensification to mitigate psychosocial risks.
•	Plagiarism and assistance misuse detection: Monitoring, identifying, and addressing plagiarism and misuse of artificial intelligence assistance.
•	Authenticity and fraud evaluation: Utilizing AI content Detectors or AI Detection Systems to verify the authenticity of term papers and prevent violations of the educational pact.
•	Protecting against false accusations: Guaranteeing detection tool reliability to prevent a serious unfounded accusation of fraud.
•	Data privacy and algorithmic bias: Addressing data privacy concerns, algorithmic biases, and implementing robust data security measures.
•	Perceived fairness and dispute reduction: Exploring the moderating effect of engineering ethics on the relationship between perceived fairness (decision, procedural, quality of treatment) and dispute potential.
•	De-colonizing academic research: Disrupting patterns of symbolic violence and eliminating institutional racism within academic journals and funding bodies.
•	Ensuring culturally secure environments: Ensuring ethical research with Indigenous communities by establishing culturally secure research environments.
•	Indigenous core values: Prioritizing ethical values of respect, relationships, advocacy, reciprocity, time, and gratitude.
•	Relational trust building: Underpinning research partnerships with relational values of trust, respect, and reciprocity.
