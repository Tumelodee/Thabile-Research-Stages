8. Data Collection
•	Database search extraction: Querying and extracting documents from Scopus, ProQuest, or Web of Science.
•	ESL assessment instruments: Administering pre-posttest results, speaking rubrics, and self-reflection questionnaires.
•	Triangulated data collection: Administering surveys, performance tests, and analyzing tool usage records over an academic semester.
•	Online and classroom surveying: Sharing an online questionnaire electronically on social platforms or administering it in classrooms.
•	Qualitative interviews and transcription: Conducting semi-structured interviews with older adults and utilizing automatic transcription of conversations.
•	Survey-based investigation: Executing surveys to collect structured data within enterprises.
•	Questionnaire distribution: Preparing and distributing questionnaires to strategic experts.
•	World Café item documentation: Gathering and documenting single impact factor items in a collaborative workshop environment.
•	Structured interviews: Conducting structured face-to-face interviews to gather empirical data.
•	Behavioral digital asset collection: Collecting behavioral and attitudinal data from digital assets such as written communications, reports/records, model representations, and computer systems.
•	Structural modeling survey collection: Distributing field study questionnaires to contract administrators.
•	Student self-regulation surveys: Gathering insights on students' perceptions and self-regulated learning skills through course feedback.
•	Multivocal survey administration: Collecting survey data from multiple respondents across international organizations.
•	Researcher-administered surveys: Administering surveys directly to corporate real estate officers.
•	Doctoral survey and interview execution: Deploying survey questionnaires and semi-structured interviews.
•	Likert-scale rating tool deployment: Distributing a rating tool online using five-scale Likert points.
•	Pre-defined scoping data extraction: Extracting variables from eligible articles into a pre-defined data extraction table.
