10. Interpretation and Discussion of Findings
•	Mean score evaluation: Evaluating pre-posttest results to reveal significant differences of mean scores.
•	Measuring academic performance and retention: Analyzing performance tests to evaluate personalization of learning, engagement, and greater retention of information.
•	Evaluating technology impacts: Assessing the detrimental impact of technologies on psychological or cognitive skills, such as emotional intelligence.
•	Comparing performance and completion times: Observing student performance to determine comparable test marks and completion times.
•	Perceived importance correlation analysis: Analyzing Mann–Whitney results to identify perceived importance of skills and possible correlations among knowledge areas.
•	Deductive and structured diagnosis: Synthesizing structured interviews to diagnose issues and formulate general conclusions.
•	Structural equation interpretation: Evaluating structural models to determine strategic project alignment.
•	Moderating effect analysis: Assessing the moderating effect of variables (e.g., engineering ethics) on operational relationships.
•	Mixed-methods evaluation of competitive advantage: Applying the mixed methods approach to evaluate framework impact on sustainable competitive advantage (core competencies, technical capabilities, social capital).
•	Dependability, reflexivity, and transference: Interpreting qualitative results to facilitate reflexivity, foster dependability of outcomes, and achieve transference in findings.
•	Mapping reporting impacts: Mapping and synthesizing the reporting impacts of patient and public involvement (PPI) in medical research.
