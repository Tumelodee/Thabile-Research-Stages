3. Stakeholder Analysis and Requirements Gathering
•	Stakeholder management tailoring: Implementing stakeholder management processes across nine knowledge areas tailored to organization sizes.
•	Stakeholder engagement workflows: Integrating production, sustainability, and regulatory teams to align goals in complex circular economy frameworks.
•	Stakeholder sentiment analysis: Deploying Natural Language Processing (NLP), Support Vector Machines (SVM), Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM), and the VADER model to automate and analyze textual stakeholder feedback.
•	Mapping community needs: Capturing stakeholder feedback to align project objectives with environmental, social, and economic community priorities.
•	Subject-object stakeholder mapping: Recasting key stakeholders as active decision-making "subjects" of management rather than passive execution "objects".
•	Collaborative brainstorming: Utilizing structured group brainstorming and idea-CRE / Commercial Real Estate project-based managementation techniques to capture operational requirements.
