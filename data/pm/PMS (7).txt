7. Risk Management and Procurement Strategy
•	HSE / HEALTH, SAFE / SCALED AGILE FRAMEWORKTY, AND ENVIRONMENTAL MANAGEMENT risk integration: Identifying, analyzing, and managing health, SAFe / Scaled Agile Frameworkty, and environmental risks within strategic project management.
•	Ecological SAFe / Scaled Agile Frameworkty forecasting: Applying predictive models to mitigate ecological hazards and prevent traffic-flow critical situations.
•	Contract management: Administering procurement and supplier contracts using the Smart Contract Project Management Framework (SCPMF / SMART CONTRACT PROJECT MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK).
•	Automated contract execution: Utilizing blockchain and smart contracts to enable immutable record-keeping and automated transaction execution.
•	Claim administration: Assessing decision outcome fairness, procedural fairness, and quality of treatment in claim administration to reduce dispute potential.
•	Supply chain risk mitigation: Structuring lean workflows to secure and manage the supply chain of reusable materials.
