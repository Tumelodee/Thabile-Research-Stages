12. Dissemination of Research Findings
•	Informing public science policy: Utilizing R&D project outcomes to support the implementation of public science policies.
•	Proposing future research trajectories: Highlighting keyword co-occurrences to pinpoint future research possibilities.
•	DSR communication phase: Executing the communication phase of the design science research framework.
•	Layperson dissemination: Consulting with advisory partners to disseminate results in a format that is accessible and understood by a layperson to communicate with diverse communities.
•	Academic journal publication: Publishing scoping reviews and empirical papers in peer-reviewed scientific journals.
•	Conference dissemination: Disseminating research findings at local, national, and international academic conferences.
