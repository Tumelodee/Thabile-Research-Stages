5. Schedule Estimation and Milestone Planning
•	Milestone tracking: Organizing a project management framework to support a structured, phased delivery.
•	Joint timetable design: Designing detailed joint timetables to synchronize calendars and reconcile contrasting institutional demands.
•	Activity sequencing: Sequencing tasks to manage complexity, ambiguity, and time constraints in large-scale engineering.
•	Digital-twin simulation and deduction: Running construction process simulations to dynamically model, analyze, and forecast schedules.
•	Schedule and cost analysis: Utilizing predictive models to perform continuous schedule and cost analysis.
