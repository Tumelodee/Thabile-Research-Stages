8. Team Mobilization and Communication Planning
•	HR management and organizational culture: Aligning team behaviors with corporate culture and HR-management success factors.
•	Multi-cultural team mobilization: Applying talent management practices to enhance the effectiveness of multinational and multi-cultural project teams.
•	Virtual team setup: Coordinating virtual teams across distributed global regions through structured joint communication pathways.
•	Communication management tailoring: Customizing communication plans across classical and agile project categories.
•	Project role alignment: Mapping project roles and system architectures to facilitate workflow integration.
•	Specific training: Allocating resources for individual training to properly absorb complex frameworks (like SAFe / Scaled Agile Framework).
